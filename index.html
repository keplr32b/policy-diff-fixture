Northstar Notes Privacy Notice

Effective date: September 28, 2026

Information we collect

Northstar Notes collects only the email address that a user provides when requesting account support.

How we use information

Support email addresses are used only to respond to user support requests. We do not sell personal data and do not share support email addresses with advertising partners.

Retention

Support messages are retained for no longer than 90 days and are then deleted.

User rights

Users may request deletion of their support information at any time by contacting support@northstar-notes.example.

Disputes

This notice does not require mandatory arbitration. Users retain the right to pursue available dispute-resolution options.
